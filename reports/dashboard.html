What makes a song rise?

Finding 1

Release age sets the pace

Each dot is a song. The vertical axis shows today's streams as a share of everything the song has ever streamed, which measures how fast it is still growing. Growth drops steadily for about three years after release, then levels off. The black line is the expected pace for a song of that age; everything below compares songs to it.

How this was built

Data

    Limits

    • Every song here already has more than 100M streams. These results describe hits, not songs in general.
    • One snapshot in time. "Growth" is today's streams relative to the all-time total, not a measured trend over weeks.
    • Genre comes from the album on Deezer and is coarse. "Pop" covers a lot of ground.
    • A few release dates may still be reissue dates where no original release was on record.
    Sources: Spotify stream counts (source CSV), Deezer, MusicBrainz, ReccoBeats. Model: ordinary least squares on log growth with a spline for release age, standard errors clustered by artist, Holm-adjusted p-values.
